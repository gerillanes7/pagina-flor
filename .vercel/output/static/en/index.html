About

Florencia

Florencia paints from her studio. Here is how she works and why what leaves the studio stays in the studio until it is finished.

I work in oil and acrylic on canvas, and sometimes on board. I paint almost every day; what leaves the studio stays in the studio until it is finished.

The pieces you see here are the ones I have decided to show. There is no inventory: each work is one of one.

If something interests you, write. If you want to talk about a commission, write. I am direct; let us both be.

— Florencia, 22 September 2026
