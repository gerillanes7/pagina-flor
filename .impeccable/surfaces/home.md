# Surface brief — Home

<!-- impeccable:surface-schema 1 -->

## Mode

Experience. The visitor is inside the work; the interface recedes.

## Audience, job, action

- Audience: collectors scanning a body of work, prospective clients considering a commission, gallery curators and editors, casual visitors from Instagram/search/referral.
- Job: feel the painter's body of work, understand her practice, reach her to buy or commission.
- Primary action: choose one of three rooms (Obra, Carta, Contacto); each room opens with a horizontal painted panel that is itself the door.

## Proof, content, constraints

- Real paintings: absent. Placeholder SVGs ship at production fidelity, clearly authored as placeholders.
- Painter's bio, CV, press: absent. Section placeholders stay empty; nothing fabricated.
- Bilingual ES/EN via per-route URLs; language switch sits top-right as a utility, always visible.
- Self-hosted faces only; system fonts are not the display voice.
- No invented commercial claims (no prices listed, no collectors named).
- No literal workshop metaphor: the home carries no studio scene, no canvas stacks, no brushes-in-a-jar.

## Direction

**Galería Horizontal — Horizontal Gallery.** A translation of the Ximena Agra pattern for Florencia.

## Memorable moment

The first viewport is the painter's name in a humanist cursive — the mark of a working painter, not a marketplace — sitting on pure white. The horizontal nav sits below in ink, its active link underlined in dusty rose powder (`#D3B9B9`); the language switch sits top-right as a small utility. The visitor then scrolls through three painted panels — Obra, Carta, Contacto — each a full-bleed horizontal slice of artwork, each labeled in handwritten script dead center on the painted surface. Clicking a panel opens the corresponding room.

## Unresolved

- Real paintings, bio, statement, CV, press, contact endpoint — to be supplied by the painter.
- Specific studio location and hours — to be supplied.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The home is a walk through the painter's studio, where each room is a painted panel on the wall. The signature is the entrance sign; the painted bands are the hallway; the contact door is the exit. The interface recedes; the paintings are the page.
OWN-WORLD: Pure white ground (#FFFFFF), with a single accent — dusty rose powder (#D3B9B9) — for the active nav underline, the section rules, the link color (in a darker sibling shade that reads on white), the "current work" status, the focus halo, and the primary button background. Warm ink text (#1F1414) on white. Painterly horizontal panels — full-bleed SVG banner artworks at 16:9 wide, with handwritten section names floating dead-center on each painted surface. Three type voices only: handwritten cursive (the painter's mark and the section names), tracked uppercase grotesk (the centered nav), humanist sans (the body, the form, the page titles). No radius. No mono. No hero metric.
STORY: Visitor lands, sees "Florencia" in script centered at the top on white, the horizontal nav in ink below (with the active link picking up a pink underline), the language switch top-right. Three painted panels follow — Obra, Carta, Contacto. Each panel is a horizontal slice of artwork. Each panel is itself a door to that room. The visitor never wonders if this is a marketplace; the painter's hand wrote the room labels.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Centered signature "Florencia" in Petit Formal Script at ~5.2rem, sitting on white. Below: a small uppercase sub-tag ("Estudio y obra · Madrid" / "Studio and work · Madrid") in tracked grotesk. Below: the horizontal nav in Karla uppercase, letter-spacing 0.4em, ink; the active link picks up a pink underline that animates in from the left. Top-right: the ES / EN switch in the same tracked register. Below the nav: the first painted panel "Obra" — a full-bleed horizontal artwork (16:9) with "Obra" in handwritten script dead center. Nav links remain visible by translating into the white ground above the first panel.
FORM: Galería Horizontal — Horizontal Gallery. Translation of the Ximena Agra reference: each page section is a painted band that fills the viewport width; the bands stack; the type on each band is the section name in handwritten script, centered. The painter's signature is the only mark; the painted panels do the rest.
FINISH: "unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance."
